CN Systems Desk

CN Systems Desk is a private desktop tool that The CN Systems uses to manage its own Gmail accounts and Google Calendars from one window. It runs only on the owner's own computers. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

The tool can read, draft, send, label, archive and move mail to the trash, and read calendars. Sending and deleting can require a password set by the owner.
