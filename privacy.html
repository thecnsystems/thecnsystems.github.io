Privacy policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

CN Systems Desk is a private tool that The CN Systems uses with its own Google accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What the tool accesses

With the account owner's permission, the tool accesses Gmail messages, attachments' names, drafts, labels and mail settings, and Google Calendar calendars and events.

Where data is kept

The tool runs on the owner's own computers and has no server of its own. Google sign-in keys are stored on those computers, encrypted with Windows. An activity history records the action, the account and the result, but not mail content. Mail and calendar content is fetched from Google when needed and is not kept in a database.

Who receives data

Data is not sold and is not used for advertising. It is shared with these services only:

  • Google, to carry out the requested Gmail and Calendar actions. If the owner chooses to speak a password, Chrome sends the audio to Google to turn it into text.
  • Anthropic, when the owner asks the Claude AI assistant to work on mail or calendar content. That content is sent to Claude to complete the request.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account permissions page or with the Remove button in the tool. Removing an account from the tool does not delete its mail.
